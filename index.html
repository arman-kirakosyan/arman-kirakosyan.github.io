About

For 18 months I ran the social media channels of a university department. For nine years I officiated soccer. Both came down to the same thing: understand the audience in front of you, make the call, and be ready to defend it.

I am a marketing graduate based in Marquette, Michigan. At Northern Michigan University’s International Programs Office I directed and created content for prospective and current international students, developed campaigns end to end — concept, copywriting, graphic design, and video editing — and handled the correspondence, scheduling, and student records that keep a university department running.

Alongside that I served on the executive board of the Refugee Outreach Collective chapter at NMU as Treasurer, managing chapter finances, banking, and fundraising, and creating the digital content that promoted our campaigns.

The nine years came with the United States Soccer Federation, where I made and defended real-time decisions in front of players, coaches, and spectators, and de-escalated confrontations without losing the room.

I am now looking for an entry-level marketing or sales role in the Marquette area.
